🎯 Why Recall is Paramount in Healthcare

In clinical cardiac diagnostics, a False Negative means a patient with severe cardiovascular risk is mistakenly discharged as healthy. By prioritizing high Recall (90.91%), KNN captures over 90% of genuine positive cases.

📐 Why KNN Excelled on This Dataset

Cardiovascular tabular features (such as blood pressure, cholesterol, and maximum heart rate) form tight localized topological clusters in standardized Euclidean space. KNN effectively identifies patient proximity to historical positive clusters without overfitting.

⚡ Feature Preprocessing & Scaling

Because KNN is distance-based, categorical features were standardized with LabelEncoder and continuous biomarkers with StandardScaler ($\mu=0, \sigma=1$). This prevents high-magnitude features like cholesterol from skewing Euclidean distances.
